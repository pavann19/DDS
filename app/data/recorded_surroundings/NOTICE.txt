Recorded OpenStreetMap surroundings

Source: existing genuine DDS OpenStreetMap API/Overpass cache responses.
Each JSON file is copied byte for byte. Original retrieval timestamps, OSM
object timestamps/versions, geometry and modeled-height/width provenance are
preserved. SHA-256 and byte sizes are pinned in manifest.json and its digest
is pinned in the loader. Coverage is partial and limited to the default
validated mission corridor. These records are NOT live free-space evidence.
